Retake Studio

Everything for the demo video: the stage, a teleprompter that floats over your windows, and Retake recording this tab with your camera.

  1. Make this window full screen (F11, or the button below).
  2. Open the teleprompter and put it just under your webcam.
  3. Press Start recording and choose this tab.
  4. Read each line. Press → or Space (or a presentation clicker) for the next one. ← goes back.
